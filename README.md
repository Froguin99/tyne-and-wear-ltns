# Low Traffic Neighbourhood plausibility in Tyne & Wear

Interactive map of how LTN-like every neighbourhood is across the five local authorities of Tyne & Wear
(Newcastle upon Tyne, Gateshead, North Tyneside, South Tyneside and Sunderland).

**[Open the map](https://froguin99.github.io/tyne-and-wear-ltns/)**

Each neighbourhood (an area bounded by main roads) is scored 0&ndash;100 on how much its features indicate it is likely
to be a Low Traffic Neighbourhood, combining three measures:

- **Through routes** - can drivers cut through between the surrounding main roads, and is it quicker than going round?
- **Modal filtering** - bollards, street continuations, cycle contraflows and bus gates per length of street
- **Permeability** - how much shorter trips within the neighbourhood are by bike or on foot than by car

Pick an area from the panel (or click a neighbouring area on the map), hover a neighbourhood for its scores, and use
the legend to switch layers on and off. Add `#area=sunderland` (etc.) to the address to link to a specific area.
